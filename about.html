We test everything on our own feet first

GRIT started as a two-person operation sourcing sneakers from local runners and street skaters. It's grown, but the standard hasn't changed.

From one shelf to a real boutique

GRIT opened with a single rack of sneakers picked by two friends who couldn't find shoes that survived their own routines — one training for a marathon, the other grinding curbs on a board every evening.

Every pair we stock gets worn by someone on the team before it's listed. If it doesn't hold up to a real run, a real game, or a real slide down a handrail, it doesn't make the cut.

We're still small enough to know our regulars by name, and we plan to keep it that way.
